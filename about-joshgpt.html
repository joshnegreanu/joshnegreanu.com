About JoshGPT.
JoshGPT is my own personal project, a small-scale LLM chatbot built and trained entirely from scratch. While early versions will utilize autoregressive generation paradigms, future versions will explore diffusion-based language modeling and reasoning.

The project is focused on building a deep understanding of the inner workings of language models, and exploring novel architectures and training paradigms for next generation models.

Please feel free to view the Github organization. You can also chat with JoshGPT.

Starting pretraining of joshgpt-beta

April 14, 2026
I have started pretraining the second version of JoshGPT, dubbed joshgpt-beta. This version is larger, with 24 transformer layers, 12 attention heads, and employing attention residuals across each transformer layer. I am pretraining on the HuggingFaceTB corpus dataset cosmopedia-v2, which is a large, diverse, and clean synthetic dataset for pretraining. This dataset contains roughly 30 billion tokens; I am unsure if I will be able to fully train on this dataset or simply a subset of it.

I am training on a single H100 GPU, and am using a context window of 2048 tokens, a batch size of 32 sequences, and a learning rate of 3e-4 with cosine annealing. This should hopefully provide me with a very smooth training procedure, and I am excited to see how the model performs as training progresses.

After pretraining, I plan to finetune the model on a variety of chatbot tasks via the smoltalk datatset, again provided by HuggingFaceTB. This dataset contains a variety of high quality chatbot conversations, and should provide a good finetuning dataset for the model.

My eventual plans are to also employ RLHF using DPO, though this can wait until after the initial pretraining and finetuning is complete.

I have learned a lot about this process from my first attempt at building JoshGPT, dubbed joshgpt-alpha. While this model generated natural language, it performed poorly on tasks and was difficult to converse with. I hope that with the improvements I have made to the architecture and training procedure, joshgpt-beta will be a much more capable and engaging model.
